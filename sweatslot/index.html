🏋️ Smart Workout Planning

Plan Workouts Without Cluttering Your Calendar

SweatSlot lets you define your workout types and simple rules, then drag them straight onto your calendar. No more mental math about whether you've hit your training targets this week.

TestFlight beta

Everything You Need

🏋️

Custom Workout Types

Define your own workout types — running, cycling, strength, swimming, or anything else you train — each with a set duration.

⚖️

Min/Max Rules

Set simple limits like "max one long run per week" or "at least 2 strength sessions per week," and let SweatSlot keep you on track.

🔁

Flexible Frequency

Rules aren't limited to weekly. Set something like "at least one swim every 2 weeks" for lower-frequency training too.

📅

Calendar-Aware

Reads your existing calendar and shows a week, two-week, or month view, so you always plan around what's already there.

🖐️

Drag to Plan

Drag your workout types straight onto the calendar to fill out your training schedule in seconds, not tedious manual entries.

📱

Native iOS Experience

Supports light and dark mode, iCloud sync across all your devices, and feels right at home on your iPhone.
