Privacy Policy

Last updated: [date]. Applies to all Marvey Studios apps: BrickLog, StrideLog, SweatSlot, and Runway.

Overview

Marvey Studios apps don't run their own servers to store your collection data. Your data — LEGO sets, running shoes, workouts, and so on — syncs through your own personal iCloud account via Apple's CloudKit. Marvey Studios does not have access to it, and it is never sold or shared with advertisers.

Data used by each app

BrickLog

When you look up a LEGO set, its set number is sent to the Rebrickable API to fetch set and piece details. If you add your own Rebrickable API key, it's stored locally on your device, not on any Marvey Studios server.

StrideLog

If you choose to connect Strava, StrideLog uses Strava's OAuth to read your running activity and shoe data. This connection is optional — the app remains fully usable without it — and can be revoked at any time from Settings, both in the app and in your Strava account.

Analytics and tracking

Marvey Studios apps do not use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

Your rights

Since your collection data lives in your own iCloud account, you can access, export, or delete it at any time directly within the app or by managing your iCloud storage. For data handled by third-party services (Rebrickable, Strava), refer to their own privacy policies for how they handle your account data.

Children's privacy

Marvey Studios apps are not directed at children under 13 (or the relevant age in your region), and do not knowingly collect personal data from children.
