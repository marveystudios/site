About

Marvey Studios started the way most good ideas do: out of pure, mild frustration. I'm Anneleen and I'm the kind of person who loves tracking and organizing things a little too much, but I could never find an app that did it exactly the way I wanted. So, naturally, I decided to just make my own.

By day I'm an accountant, so "coding background" is not really my thing, although it had always piqued my interest. I'd tried to learn how to build apps a few times over the years, but it never quite stuck, until AI tools like Claude came along and finally made it click. Turns out all I needed was the right sidekick.

I'm based in Belgium, and I share this corner of the internet with my two dogs, Harvey and Marvel. Put their names together, and yep, that's where "Marvey" comes from.
