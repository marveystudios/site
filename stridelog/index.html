👟 For Runners

Track Your Running Shoes

StrideLog helps you track your running shoes, monitor mileage through Strava, add more info and get fun statistics. The simple way to manage your running gear.

Coming Soon to the App Store

Everything You Need

👟

Shoe Tracking

Log all your running shoes in one place. Track brand, model, and when you first laced them up.

📏

Mileage Tracking

Know exactly how many kilometers — or miles — are on each pair. Never run a shoe into the ground by accident again.

🔗

Powered by Strava

Link your Strava account to automatically import your shoes and keep your mileage up to date after every run.

🗂️

Reserve Shoes

Track shoes you own but haven't added to Strava yet. Keep your full collection in one place.

🏁

Retirement Tracking

Retire worn-out shoes and keep a record of their lifetime mileage. A fitting send-off for faithful companions.

📱

Native iOS Experience

Supports light and dark mode, iCloud sync across all your devices, and feels right at home on your iPhone.
